What moves spending

What the last five years of Steam sales show. The number badge is the size of the effect, in percent or points versus the normal trend.

+6.6Steam seasonal sales: more skins sold, at slightly lower prices
Across 20 Steam seasonal sales since 2021, basket volume ran +6.6 pp vs trend during the sale (90% of sales above trend) while prices ran -3.0 pp. Strongest in the Summer Sale for skins (+12.2 pp volume, -4.6 pp price). Consistent with people selling items for Steam Wallet money to buy discounted games. Liquid weeks, but slightly cheaper ones.
High confidence
+7.2Christmas week lifts activity, but December as a whole is weak (capsules especially)
Dec 24 to Jan 1: volume +7.2 pp vs trend in 5/5 years (100% above), prices flat (-0.7 pp). But December overall runs -7.4% below its yearly trend (median, 0/5 years above). Capsules run -25.6% (0/5 above). Early December is a lull before the Winter Sale.
High confidence
-13.2Two reliable lulls: January and July-August
Median month vs yearly trend: January -7.5% (capsules -23.6%, 0/6 years above), July -12.2%, August -13.2% (0/6 years above; capsules -29.3%).
High confidence
-70.3New Major capsules collapse for ~2-4 months before bottoming
Across 35 Major sticker/autograph capsules (2020 RMR to Budapest 2025), the median price vs the week-2 price was -32% at day 30, -70% at day 60 and -64% at day 180. The first-year low had a median of -78%, reached around day 157 (median). Don't bulk-buy a new Major capsule in its first two months.
High confidence
+6.9New case/terminal drops pull activity up
In the first week after 6 case/terminal releases, volume ran +6.9 pp vs trend (83% above).
High confidence
+89.2Valve economy changes dwarf every calendar effect
First-week volume vs trend: Counter-Strike 2 launches +28 pp; Trade-up update +89 pp. Calendar effects are single-digit pp. A Valve economy patch can flip any seasonal call overnight, so check patch notes before acting on a window.
High confidence

Medium confidence
+14.7Weekly and monthly rhythm: Friday-Saturday (UTC) and the first days of the month are busiest
UTC weekdays vs weekly average: Fri +6.1%, Sat +7.1%, Sun +2.7%, Thu +0.9% vs Mon -7.3%, Tue -8.9%. UTC Friday-Saturday is about Thursday 7 PM to Saturday 7 PM CT (6 PM during standard time). Days 1-4 of the month run +2.8% vs days 14-21 at -2.7% (payday/allowance timing). Small effects, but consistent across about 2,000 days.
Medium confidence
Player counts do not drive month-to-month spending
Monthly changes in CS2 average players (SteamCharts) are uncorrelated with basket units sold (r about 0.04, 2021-2026). Players fell from 1.08M (Feb 2026) to 0.81M (Sep 2026), alongside a weaker market. Treat players as a slow background signal, not a timing tool.
Medium confidence
No reliable effect: hardware launches, big game releases, Next Fest, Prime Day, Lunar New Year, 11.11, tax-refund weeks
Volume vs trend during the event window: Valve hardware launch +1.5 pp (n=6); Big game release / pre-order +1.7 pp (n=10); Steam Next Fest -0.5 pp (n=10); Amazon Prime events +1.6 pp (n=4); Lunar New Year +1.7 pp (n=6); Singles' Day 11.11 -4.8 pp (n=5); US tax refund weeks +2.8 pp (n=3). All are within normal week-to-week noise (about ±9 pp).
Medium confidence
